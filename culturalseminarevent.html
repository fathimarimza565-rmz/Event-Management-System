Breaking the Silence:
A Cultural Seminar on Gender-Based Violence

Join us for a thought-provoking and empowering evening dedicated to raising awareness, challenging societal norms, and inspiring change. This seminar brings together voices from diverse backgrounds to explore the cultural roots of gender-based violence, its impact on individuals and communities, and the steps we must take toward a more just and safe society. Through storytelling, panel discussions, artistic expression, and survivor testimonies, we aim to foster understanding and empathy.

Together, we can create a culture of respect and accountability. This event is not just a seminar — it’s a call to action. It’s a space for open dialogue, healing, and education, where everyone has a role to play in ending gender-based violence. Whether you're attending to learn, support, or speak out, your presence matters. Let’s stand in solidarity, amplify marginalized voices, and work toward lasting cultural change.
