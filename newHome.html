About Our Platform

The Event Management System of the College of Technology is a centralized platform developed to keep students informed about institutional events. It provides timely updates on upcoming activities and maintains a comprehensive record of completed events.

Our Mission

To provide a reliable and accessible platform that bridges the communication gap between the institution and students, enabling seamless access to event information and encouraging greater involvement in campus life.

Our Vision

To become an integral part of the college ecosystem by fostering a more connected, informed, and engaged student community through efficient event management and communication tools.

Meet The Team

This platform is developed and maintained by a dedicated team of students from the College of Technology, committed to enhancing the digital experience on campus. With a shared vision of improving communication and accessibility, the team combines technical expertise and a passion for innovation.
